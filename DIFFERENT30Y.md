# 30 Years of Heritage

We are unique within the hosting industry for many important reasons. Our 15 years of Ægir-based hosting, plus earlier experience with Adgrafix (the first company to offer a control panel for website management in 1995), have helped shape what makes us different today.

Fun fact: Robert “Bo” Bennett, the founder of Adgrafix, started as a self-taught programmer and created the world's first web hosting control panel entirely in Perl. We continued to build on that foundation for almost ten years, adding shopping cart and marketing tools and the early BOA version -- everything in Perl. Why not in PHP? Because PHP was still in its infancy, since its early prototype 1.0 was released in June 1995 by Rasmus Lerdorf, while Perl 4 had been out since 1991 and was already a stable, mature, widely adopted language used both for server tools, websites backend and frontend.

## Focus, focus, focus — like no one else

Our website has been running on Grav CMS for years, and for most of those years we called our hosting 100% Drupal-focused. Said plainly, that always undersold it. What stayed constant was a standard, not a product: software that is open, answerable to its owner, fast, structured, and built to outlast fashion. Drupal has served that standard best, for the longest stretch, and it remains the heart of what we host, from Pressflow 6 and Drupal 7 through Drupal 11 and Drupal CMS.

Today the same standard admits three more families: **Backdrop CMS**, the Drupal 7 continuity path; **Grav CMS**, which also runs our own documentation; and **Textpattern**, the kindred platform we chose before Drupal. Every one of them ships as a ready [**Ægir Platform**](https://docs.boa.io/platforms), installed, cloned, backed up and restored with the same panel tasks. Others widened by adding WordPress and a dozen other platforms; we add a platform only when it meets the standard, and then we host it like no one else. That’s how you become **best-in-class, fastest, and most trusted** by the open-source community.

## Technological sovereignty

We take **Open Source seriously** — it’s not a buzzword for us. It’s about freedom from corporate control. Here's a short look back at our 15-year Ægir journey and 19 years with Drupal.

Our BOA system is the most complete solution of its kind. It was designed from the ground up to make Drupal hosting **faster, easier, safer, and more efficient** — especially for teams that don’t want to get lost in DevOps — and today it does the same for Backdrop, Grav and Textpattern. It was always built on Debian OS, but we never accepted systemd, imposed by Red Hat. After years of battling, we switched fully to **Devuan** — the true Debian fork without systemd, built by Debian veterans.

At the same time, we stayed away from MariaDB, because it drifted away from MySQL compatibility and became vendor-focused. Instead, we adopted **Percona** — a truly open, compatible, and community-aligned database option. The same thing happened with Redis — the fast cache-in-memory server. As soon as Redis switched to a restrictive corporate license, we moved to the open-source **Valkey** fork.

Bottom line: BOA is **architected around technological freedom and independence**. We work for you — not for Oracle, Red Hat, investors or proprietary agendas. That matters, especially for universities, NGOs, and businesses who rely on no-compromise sovereignty.
