Here is what this one means for you and your sites.

----------------------------------------
BACKUPS THAT MEAN EXACTLY WHAT THEY SAY
----------------------------------------

The Backup button in your control panel now offers three clearly named choices -- a database snapshot, a files snapshot, or the complete restorable backup -- and the complete one is pre-selected, so the natural single click gives you a backup you can restore from. And from now on a database-only or files-only snapshot is labelled as such in your backup lists, so a partial backup can no longer be mistaken for a complete one.

----------------------------------------
RESTORE BRINGS BACK EXACTLY WHAT YOU SAVED
----------------------------------------

Restore a complete backup and your site returns to precisely the moment you saved it, files and database together. Restore a partial one, new or old, and the panel says plainly which half came back and which was left as it was.

----------------------------------------
A QUIETER, SHARPER SHIELD IN FRONT OF YOUR SITES
----------------------------------------

Some scrapers spread their traffic across thousands of addresses to stay below every radar, and the interactive links they lean on hardest -- social-login windows and flag buttons, which have to run your site's code on every hit -- are now answered at the front door before any of your site's code runs, while your genuine visitors carry on exactly as before. The protection layer also recognises and reports the wider campaign behind such traffic, so it can be dealt with instead of showing up as unexplained load.

----------------------------------------
FRESHER FOUNDATIONS FOR YOUR NEXT PROJECT
----------------------------------------

Drupal 10.6 and 11.4 are both ready to use as complete platform sets, and OpenCulturas moves to 3.0.5.
