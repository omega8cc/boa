It arrives on BOA's new rhythm of smaller, more frequent updates, so improvements reach you sooner.

@=> Never lose track of an interrupted task again

If a migrate, clone or restore is ever cut short, BOA no longer leaves the half-finished pieces lying around out of sight: it notices exactly what was left behind and keeps a record for you. A new leftover import task surfaces each one in your control panel as a clearly marked, disabled placeholder site, so you can see at a glance what is there -- and when you are sure you no longer need it, a companion leftover purge task removes that placeholder cleanly in a single step, including any stray copy of its database. It works the same way for both Drupal and Backdrop sites.

@=> A smoother move from Drupal 7 to Backdrop

The task that carries a Drupal 7 site over to Backdrop now runs more smoothly and lands more dependably: a class of intermittent hiccups during conversion and migration has been ironed out, so the converted copy comes up clean and ready for you to review. Once a conversion finishes, its menus and admin links are rebuilt properly as part of the run, so the new Backdrop site behaves exactly as you expect from the very first click.

@=> A rock-steady Backdrop admin bar

Backdrop's admin bar -- the management strip across the top of your site -- is now dependable on every page across all your Backdrop sites. Where it could occasionally flicker out of view, it now loads reliably every time, so the controls you run your sites with are always right where you left them.

@=> A tidier Backdrop task list

Working with Backdrop in the panel is cleaner this time round: task names are shorter and easier to read, the action buttons sit neatly on a single line, and upgrade tasks that simply do not apply to a given site are no longer offered -- less clutter, and fewer wrong turns.
