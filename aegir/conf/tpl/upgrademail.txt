We are pleased to inform you that your Ægir instance has been successfully upgraded to [boa.version]

@=> Stronger protection at the edge

This release adds a new, fine-grained policy for AI bots and crawlers: aggressive training scrapers are turned away by default, while ordinary search and assistant traffic is allowed and rate-limited fairly. BOA now also recognises the real visitor behind Cloudflare, so abuse protection, rate limits, and logs reflect the actual client rather than the network in front of it -- and repeat offenders are blocked right at the web server. Your sites continue to serve normal visitors exactly as before, with no action required on your part.

@=> Control AI bots and visitor access per site

You can adjust the AI bot policy for any individual site -- for example allow AI training crawlers, or block AI search and assistant fetchers -- and you can restrict a site to specific visitor IP addresses (handy for an intranet, staging, or an admin-only area). Both are simple per-site control files, picked up automatically within about two minutes. Step-by-step guides:

  AI bots, per site:   https://github.com/omega8cc/boa/blob/5.x-dev/docs/AI-POLICY-USER.md
  IP access, per site: https://github.com/omega8cc/boa/blob/5.x-dev/docs/IP-ACCESS-USER.md

@=> Important if you use CiviCRM

For security reasons, BOA no longer automatically trusts the command files that CiviCRM ships inside each site. Your CiviCRM sites continue to run completely normally under PHP -- visitors and administrators will see no difference at all. However, the behind-the-scenes Ægir maintenance tasks for a CiviCRM site (such as verify and migrate) now require a one-time control file to be enabled first. Enabling it requires root access and is not turned on by default. If you run your own BOA server with root access, create the file /data/conf/<octopus-user>_civicrm.txt for each affected account. If your server is managed by us, simply reply and let us know you use CiviCRM, and we will enable it for your account.

@=> If you clone or rename a site

To prevent a renamed or cloned site from inheriting the original site's TLS certificate, BOA now automatically disables Encryption on the new site. If the new address should serve HTTPS, simply re-enable Encryption for it in your Ægir control panel and a fresh certificate will be issued. Managed customers: just let us know and we will take care of it.
