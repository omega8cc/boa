We are pleased to inform you that your Ægir instance has been successfully upgraded to [boa.version]

Here is what this one means for you and your sites.

----------------------------------------
EVERY BACKUP YOU START IS NOW THE RESTORABLE KIND
----------------------------------------

The previous release taught the Backup button to pre-select the complete, restorable mode. This one completes the promise everywhere else: backups started from the sites list or on a schedule now produce the same complete archive as a panel click -- files and database together, ready to restore.

----------------------------------------
THE SAFETY NET UNDER RESTORE GOT REAL
----------------------------------------

Before every Restore, Ægir quietly saves a copy of the site as it stands. That automatic safety copy is now itself a complete, restorable backup, database included -- so even if a restore takes a wrong turn, the way back is a backup you can actually use.

----------------------------------------
EVERY BACKUP NOW PROVES ITSELF
----------------------------------------

Every backup verifies its database export before it is listed, so what you see in your backup lists is what you can restore from. A restore states plainly what it brought back, every time. And a stale exclusion marker left behind by an interrupted run now clears itself instead of quietly shrinking every later backup.

----------------------------------------
A SMALL TRAP IN THE SITE FORM IS GONE
----------------------------------------

Pasting a copied web address as a new site's name could leave a trailing slash in the stored domain, with confusing results. The address is now cleaned at the door, and genuine subdirectory sites like example.com/shop keep working exactly as before. If a site created that way ever ended up redirecting to itself endlessly, it needs a one-off cleanup by your server administrator -- new sites are safe from here on.

----------------------------------------
FRESHER FOUNDATIONS FOR YOUR NEXT PROJECT
----------------------------------------

A new Drupal 7 platform is waiting for you on core 7.105.2, our own extended-support release. Verify or migrate a site onto it and a persistent database warning some sites showed clears itself on the first update run, and form validation messages keep your custom field markup intact. And your sites now sit behind a per-site ceiling on anonymous traffic surges, so a flood aimed at one site cannot crowd out the rest -- anyone already signed in is never affected.

Curious about the details? The full story of this release is at https://docs.boa.io/releases/boa-5-88-844
