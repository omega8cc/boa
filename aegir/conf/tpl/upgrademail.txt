We are pleased to inform you that your Ægir instance has been successfully upgraded to [boa.version]

@=> A sharper, smarter shield around your sites

Your sites just gained a noticeably stronger guard at the edge. BOA now spots and turns away a whole spectrum of automated abuse before it ever touches your Drupal -- botnets that hammer your login and password-reset forms, web-shell scanners hunting for a way in, and distributed scrapers that try to exhaust a server by forcing multilingual sites to translate page after page on the fly. Each of these now meets a purpose-built detector that recognises the pattern and shuts it down at the front door. And here is the part you will feel day to day: the detection was retuned so that real, lively traffic -- a burst of on-site searches, a page that suddenly gets popular -- is read as the genuine activity it is, never mistaken for an attack. The outcome is the best of both worlds: far tougher on real abuse, far gentler on your actual visitors, and a faster, steadier site for everyone browsing it.

@=> A smoother, more confident maintenance workflow on Drupal 10 and 11

If you manage your sites from the Ægir control panel, the everyday work just got genuinely more pleasant. "Lock Local Drush" is now a fast, dependable one-click task -- the targeted way to restore your platform's Drush environment after you have been working with site-local Drush or Composer, so you no longer have to reach for a full Platform Verify just to get things back in order. Run it whenever you like: it is completely safe to repeat and simply settles your platform into the state you want. "Rebuild registry" and "Flush all caches" now do precisely the right thing on modern Drupal -- rebuilding the service container and clearing caches so your changes take effect at once and your site feels crisp the moment an update lands. And when you clone or migrate a site, it comes up clean, fully refreshed, and immediately aware of any available updates, with none of the old manual fiddling. Less guesswork, fewer surprises, and more of your time spent on the work you actually enjoy.

@=> Quietly faster and more resilient underneath

Below the surface, the platform now keeps a smarter eye on its own health and shares its resources more fairly between sites, so a busy neighbour is far less likely to slow you down and your sites ride out traffic spikes and routine backups without a stumble. You will not touch a single setting -- you will simply feel a steadier, more responsive platform under everything you do.
