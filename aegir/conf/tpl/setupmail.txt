LOGGING IN
----------------------------------------

Go to https://aegir.url.name and sign in with:

username: dragon.ftp
password: FN8rXcQn

Please note: your control panel account and your SSH/FTP/SFTP account are two fully separate accounts. They only start out with the same username and password for convenience — they are not linked. We recommend changing the password on each of them separately after your first login: the control panel password from within the panel, and the SSH/FTP/SFTP password over SSH with the "passwd" command.

Forgot your control panel password later? Reset it with your email address at https://aegir.url.name/user/password

----------------------------------------
UPLOADING FILES (MODULES, THEMES, PLATFORMS)
----------------------------------------

Upload over SSH/SFTP/FTPS with the same username and password:

host: aegir.url.name
user: dragon.ftp
pass: FN8rXcQn
port: 22 (SSH/SFTP)
port: 21 (FTPS, Explicit TLS)

Type "help" once you're logged in over SSH to see the commands available to you.

----------------------------------------
YOUR DATABASES
----------------------------------------

Browse and manage them from the web, no setup required:

https://aegir.url.name/sqladmin/

Direct MySQL access over the network is closed for security — use the web tool above, or your own desktop client over an SSH tunnel.

----------------------------------------
GOING LIVE
----------------------------------------

To put a site live on your own domain, point its DNS at your server:

yourdomain.com.           IN  A      166.84.6.231
subdomain.yourdomain.com. IN  CNAME  aegir.url.name.

For quick tests you don't need DNS at all — any subdomain of aegir.url.name works straight away, for example http://atrium.aegir.url.name.
